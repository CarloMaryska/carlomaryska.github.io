Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

FitFind is provided by Carlo Maryska. This policy explains what data the app uses to scan outfits and return shoppable product links. For questions, contact hello@carlomaryska.com.

What FitFind uses

When you scan a look, FitFind sends the photo or selected video frame to our servers so garments can be detected and matched to product pages. Social links you paste or share may be fetched so the post can be loaded and a frame chosen.

An account (Sign in with Apple or Google) is required after subscribe. Saves of scanned outfits stay on this iPhone unless we later add cloud sync.

Payments

Subscriptions and credit packs are processed by Apple and RevenueCat. We do not store your full payment card details.

Your choices

You can delete your account in FitFind. That removes the FitFind account and local saves on that device. Device limits on the free tryout are kept so the tryout cannot be reused by deleting the app.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
