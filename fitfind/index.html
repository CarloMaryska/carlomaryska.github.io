How it works

From a post to the shop in five taps.

  1. 01

    Share it or paste the link

    Hit share on a TikTok or Instagram post, or paste the link into FitFind.

  2. 02

    Pick the photo

    Ten photos in one post? Choose the one with the fit you want.

  3. 03

    Choose the pieces

    FitFind marks every piece it sees. Tap to include one or skip it.

  4. 04

    Let it compare

    It searches the shops and weighs every match. Takes a few seconds.

  5. 05

    Shop the answer

    Every piece with its shop and price, marked Identical or Similar. One tap to buy.
